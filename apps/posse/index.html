Posse

A Micropub client for iPhone and iPad.

Posse publishes notes and articles straight to your own website using Micropub, signing in with IndieAuth. Your posts live on your domain — not on someone else's platform.

What Posse asks for

  • create — publish new posts
  • update — edit posts you have already published
  • delete — remove posts
  • media — upload photos to your media endpoint
  • draft — save posts without publishing them
  • profile — read your name and photo for display in the app

Where your data goes

Nowhere but your own site. Posse talks directly to the Micropub and token endpoints advertised on your homepage. Access tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. There is no Posse server and no analytics.
