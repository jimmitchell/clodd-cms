Micropub Publisher

A Micropub publishing client for macOS and iOS. It signs in to any Micropub-capable site with IndieAuth and posts notes, articles, and photos.

What it asks for

When you sign in, your own site asks whether to grant:

  • create — publish new posts and upload media
  • update — edit posts you have already published
  • delete — delete and undelete posts
  • media — upload files to your media endpoint

Your access token is stored in the system Keychain on your device. It is never sent anywhere except your own site.

This page is also the app's IndieAuth client_id. Authorization servers fetch it to identify the app and to verify its callback URL.
